2. Proposed Solution & Innovation

The Autonomous Multi-Role Voice Interview Committee

A next-generation platform where multiple AI specialists collaborate in real time with shared memory, dynamic follow-ups, and natural speech.

01

Autonomous Committee Turn-Taking

Rather than one chatbot pretending to be everyone, Vocalis runs distinct specialists (e.g. Marcus Vance for concurrency/kernel bottlenecks, Elena Rostova for business SLAs, David Chen for team execution). They negotiate speaking turns based on who has the sharpest follow-up.

02

Real-Time Shared Candidate Memory

All agents read and write to a unified context bus containing candidate resume metrics, previous answers, unresolved technical probes, detected contradictions, and competency progression—guaranteeing zero repeated questions.

03

Dynamic Depth & Difficulty Calibration

Analyzes candidate answers for superficial buzzwords vs. genuine architectural depth. Strong answers trigger distributed edge-case escalations (Staff/Principal tier), while struggling candidates receive structured scaffolding.

04

100% Evidence-Based Scorecard Generation

Generates post-round committee consensus scorecards with timestamped quote citations, rubric ratings, flagged architectural risks, and personalized candidate development roadmaps.

✓ Multi-Agent Handoffs ✓ Sub-100ms Interruptibility ✓ Resume Verification ✓ Zero Demographic Bias
3. Technical Architecture & End-to-End Pipeline

Full-Stack Real-Time Multi-Agent Topology

Sub-120ms total roundtrip audio loop with autonomous deliberation, shared state, and instant barge-in.

1. Ingress & VAD (<30ms) Agora SDK captures high-fidelity 48kHz audio and detects speech boundaries instantly with zero buffer bloat.
2. Committee Arbitrator (<60ms) Multi-agent orchestrator ranks candidate depth and picks the optimal specialist to probe or challenge.
3. Egress Stream (<30ms) Agora SD-RTN streams the selected interviewer's voice with spatial positioning and instant barge-in support.
4. Deep Dive: Planned Utilization of Agora Technologies

Harnessing the Full Power of Agora Real-Time AI

How Vocalis natively integrates Agora's core SDKs, Conversational AI Engine, and Global SD-RTN™ mesh.

01

Agora Voice SDK (Web & Mobile)

Ultra-low latency bidirectional audio streaming with Opus 48kHz studio audio, dynamic bitrate scaling, and seamless WebRTC fallback across all browsers.

Agora API: RtcEngine.joinChannel() + Raw Audio Observers
02

Agora SD-RTN™ Global Mesh

Software-Defined Real-Time Network guarantees <100ms global latency, 99.99% uptime, and up to 80% packet loss resilience for international candidates.

Feature: Dynamic Edge Routing & Anti-Jitter Buffer
03

Conversational AI & Barge-In VAD

Integrated Voice Activity Detection (VAD) and Acoustic Echo Cancellation (AEC). When candidate speaks, Agora immediately interrupts AI audio playback without clipping.

Feature: Sub-150ms Instant Barge-In Interrupt
04

Multi-Track Spatial Audio

Assigns separate 3D spatial audio positions to each AI interviewer (e.g. Lead Architect on Left, Product VP on Center, Security Lead on Right) replicating physical room acoustics.

Agora API: SpatialAudioEngine.updatePlayerPosition()
05

Agora AI Noise Suppression (ANS)

Deep-learning powered background noise suppression removes keyboard clicks, HVAC rumble, and room echo, ensuring clean transcripts for LLM parsing.

Feature: AI Accoustic Noise Suppression (ANS)
06

Agora Analytics & Quality Insights

Real-time telemetry tracking round audio quality, candidate speech clarity, packet loss, and latency metrics to verify fairness and prevent interview disruptions.

Feature: Agora Quality of Experience (QoE) Telemetry
